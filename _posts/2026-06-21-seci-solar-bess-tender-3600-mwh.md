SECI issued a request for selection on 19 June 2026 for 1,200 MW of solar PV hybridised with 600 MW/3,600 MWh of battery storage, connected to the ISTS network.

Pairing solar with storage lets projects supply power beyond daylight hours — a growing need for discoms and industrial buyers looking for firm, round-the-clock-style supply.
