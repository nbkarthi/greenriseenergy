India's installed solar capacity reached 168.04 GW as of 31 August 2026, with 3,445 MW added during August — up from about 2,443 MW in July.

Additions are rebounding after the post-ALMM slowdown. The sector's focus is shifting toward technology choices, storage integration and long-term project performance.
