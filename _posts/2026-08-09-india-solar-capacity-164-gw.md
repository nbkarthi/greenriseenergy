India's installed solar capacity crossed 164 GW at the end of July 2026, after roughly 2,443 MW was added during the month.

The ALMM-II waiver for projects commissioned from July 2026 fell from 75% to 50%, and is due to be phased out after July 2028. Monthly additions have cooled slightly after the pre-deadline rush, but the long-term trend remains strong.
