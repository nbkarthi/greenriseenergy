In February 2026, Prime Minister Narendra Modi hailed 30 lakh households adopting rooftop solar under the PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana.

The scheme, launched in 2024, offers central financial assistance for residential rooftop systems. Reaching 30 lakh homes shows how quickly household adoption is scaling.

For homeowners still on the fence, the message is simple: subsidy support is active, vendors are empaneled, and the process is well established. A 3kW system is the most common starting point for an average home.
