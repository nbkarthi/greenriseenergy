By late May 2026, the PM Surya Ghar scheme had solarised 40 lakh households — up from 30 lakh in February.

That is roughly 10 lakh new homes in around three months, showing that the pace of installation is accelerating.

Households considering rooftop solar can still apply through the national portal, choose an empaneled vendor and receive the subsidy after commissioning.
