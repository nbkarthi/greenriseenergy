India's solar plants generated a record 20,899.61 MU in April 2026, over 70% of all renewable generation that month. Total renewable generation rose 23% year-on-year.

Solar's share keeps growing as capacity additions outpace other sources. It also raises the importance of storage and grid flexibility to use midday power well.
