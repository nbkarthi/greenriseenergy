In August 2026, Saatvik Solar Industries signed an MoU with IPICOL, Odisha's investment promotion agency, to set up an additional 3.6 GW solar cell manufacturing facility.

With ALMM List-II in force, new cell capacity is key to easing supply constraints. More Indian cell supply should, over time, stabilise availability for rooftop and commercial projects.
