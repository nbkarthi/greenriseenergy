India is on track to commission more than 50 GWdc of solar in 2026, after adding 34 GWdc in the first half, 38% above H1 2025.

Strong utility-scale and rooftop growth, plus rising storage tenders, are shaping the market. For commercial and industrial users, the case for rooftop and solar-plus-storage keeps getting stronger.
