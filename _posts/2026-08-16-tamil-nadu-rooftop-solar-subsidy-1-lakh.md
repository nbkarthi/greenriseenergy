Tamil Nadu has announced a residential rooftop solar subsidy that works alongside PM Surya Ghar, with a maximum combined subsidy of up to ₹1,00,000 per household. The state has earmarked ₹50 crore for the scheme.

Net metering (1:1 credit for residential systems under 10 kW) continues to apply. Check eligibility and current subsidy amounts with your installer before applying, as details can change.
