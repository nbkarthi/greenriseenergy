In the latest data, Uttar Pradesh ranked first in PM Surya Ghar rooftop installations with 74,796 plants, followed by Andhra Pradesh (69,813) and Maharashtra (60,907).

State-level support, quick discom approvals and active installers make the difference. Tamil Nadu's new top-up subsidy aims to speed up adoption here too.
