ALMM List-II for solar cells came into force on 1 June 2026. The initial list enlists 13.067 GW of domestic cell capacity from nine manufacturers.

Industry has raised concerns about cell availability, especially for module makers without in-house cells. Developers rushed to commission projects before the deadline.

For rooftop buyers, expect compliant equipment to be the norm for subsidised and net-metered systems. Always check documentation before signing.
